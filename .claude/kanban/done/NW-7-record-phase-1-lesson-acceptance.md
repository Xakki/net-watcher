### Record Phase 1 synthetic lesson acceptance

**Criticality:** Medium

**TAGS:**
- lesson
- publication

**Description:**
Confirm that the first synthetic lesson meets the Phase 1 acceptance criteria from the development strategy and record the owner's decision on it.

**Problem:**
Phase 1 defined acceptance criteria for the first lesson, but no card had recorded whether the delivered lesson met them or what the owner decided.

**Impact:**
Without a recorded acceptance decision, Phase 2 planning would lack a settled basis to build on.

**Recommendation:**
Verify each Phase 1 acceptance criterion against the delivered lesson and record the owner's decision.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The lesson presents three distinct clocks and keeps claimed times separated from observable times.
- The lesson presents competing explanations and an evidence audit ahead of any conclusion.
- The lesson reaches a defensible conclusion and includes a worked self-check.
- The lesson makes no causal or intent attribution.
- Repository and site safety checks pass.

**Decisions:**
- The owner accepted the lesson as sufficiently useful and safe to continue into Phase 2.
